# Nanda Kishore's Social Media Marketing Portfolio

Welcome to your world-class marketing portfolio! This project is built with modern aesthetics, glassmorphism, and smooth scroll animations to help you land your dream internship.

## How to Customize

### 1. Upload Your Photos
- Place your profile picture or branding image in `assets/images/`.
- Update `index.html` (Around line 47) to point to your image:
  ```html
  <img src="assets/images/your-photo.jpg" alt="Nanda Kishore">
  ```

### 2. Campaign Samples
- Save your campaign creatives in `assets/images/`.
- Update each campaign placeholder in `index.html` (Search for `campaign-thumb`).
  ```html
  <div class="campaign-thumb">
      <img src="assets/images/gym-campaign.jpg" alt="Gym Campaign">
  </div>
  ```

### 3. Resume
- Place your resume (PDF format) in `assets/docs/`.
- Update the download button in `index.html` (Around line 304):
  ```html
  <a href="assets/docs/Nanda_Kishore_Resume.pdf" class="btn btn-primary" download>Download CV <i class="fa-solid fa-download"></i></a>
  ```

### 4. Technical Details
- **Styling**: All styles are in `style.css`.
- **Animations**: Powered by [ScrollReveal](https://scrollrevealjs.org/).
- **Icons**: Powered by [FontAwesome](https://fontawesome.com/).

## Final Step
Once you have uploaded your media, open `index.html` in your browser to see your stunning portfolio!
